About

A learned society is a progressive society.

Egalitarian Computers was founded on the belief that quality technology education should be accessible regardless of geography, income, or background.

Through Tech E (Tech Egalitarian), our education platform, we help learners build practical skills. Through our software engineering services, we help businesses turn ambitious ideas into useful digital products.

From your first lesson to your next launch, we believe progress starts with opportunity — and grows through knowledge.

Where it all began

Our
Story

From a local training centre to a wider world of possibilities.

Michael Nnadi and Edwin Osamezu established Egalitarian Computers in Lagos, Nigeria, starting at Oyekola Shopping Complex, Jakande Gate, Oke-Afa Isolo.

What began with local training grew into an education network serving learners in person and online, across Africa and beyond. Our teaching spans foundational IT skills, web development, data science, blockchain engineering, and more.

As our work expands, the founding principle stays the same: people deserve the opportunity to understand technology, use it confidently, and shape what comes next.
